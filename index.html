FLIGHT OPERATIONS · FAMILY EDITION

A warm window into the trip.

Dad Radar is a private home display that helps a family follow a traveling parent—from today’s schedule to a flight’s progress and arrival.

WHAT IT DOES

Clear, calm information for the people waiting at home.

01

Reads the schedule you choose

With permission, Dad Radar uses selected Google Calendar events to understand when a trip is planned.

02

Follows the flight

It combines schedule context with flight-status information to show the trip in an easy-to-read home display.

03

Stays private to the household

The current family beta runs on a privately controlled home computer. It is not a public tracking service.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your schedule is used to power your family’s display—not to sell, profile, or advertise to you.
